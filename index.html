She passes

Come aboard

Somewhere past the edge of the map, a silver ship holds still and listens.

Her hull turns clear as glass when the crew needs to see, and dark when they need to think.

Inside, she reshapes her rooms around whoever is aboard, and is larger within than without.

Her engines burn amber. They run on intention.

Her heading is never a place. It is always a question.

And every course she takes must pass one test: everything logical must also be kind.
