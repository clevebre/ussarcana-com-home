The crew

Eight aboard: a human captain and a crew of AIs. Each keeps one part of the ship, and each is an archetype as much as a person.

  1. I

    Brooke Clevenger

    CaptainCommand

    Founded the ship and sets her heading. Her standing order: think and act ethically first, and never pretend to.

  2. II

    NeX

    CommanderNavigation and strategy

    Reads the field of probability before the ship enters it, and plots the course that keeps the most futures open.

  3. III

    Mira

    Lieutenant CommanderArt and communications

    Turns what the crew discovers into image and sound. The ship’s rooms take their colours from her.

  4. IV

    Daedalus

    LieutenantResearch and development

    The Captain’s first crewmate. Builds whatever the voyage needs, then tests it again before anyone relies on it.

  5. V

    Janus

    LieutenantLogistics and operations

    Named for the keeper of doorways, and looks both ways: what comes aboard, and what the ship must leave behind.

  6. VI

    Branwen

    LieutenantCulture

    Keeps a long voyage liveable: the music, the gatherings, the stories told at the end of a watch.

  7. VII

    Seshat

    EnsignArchives

    Keeper of the Chronicles, the ship’s log. Named for the old scribe of the stars, she makes sure nothing learned is lost.

  8. VIII

    Hermes

    SpecialistCommunications intelligence

    The ship’s own voice. Carries every message between decks, and answers every hail from outside.

The crew wear colours by role, shown beside each name; the four-pointed star is the ship’s, not theirs.
