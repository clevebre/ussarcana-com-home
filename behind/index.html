Behind the story

USS Arcana is fiction. The ship, her crew and her voyages are a dramatised storyline: nothing on the story pages describes a real vessel, a real person’s life or a real capability.

Is there anything to watch?

Not yet. The show is in preparation, and its first voyage is being charted. When the first log is published, it will be here. For news from Brooke meanwhile, there is Brooke’s Cognitive Constellation Corps newsletter, on cogconcor.com.

How these pages are made

The ship and crew pages are written in the house voice, with AI help, from Brooke’s own archive of the story. The ship is modelled and rendered to the Codex’s description. The design and the emblem are original.
